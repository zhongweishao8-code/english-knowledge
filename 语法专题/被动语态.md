# 被动语态 (Passive Voice)

> 来源: 七年级下册英语教材

> 年级: 七年级下册

---

## 被动语态概览

被动语态是英语中重要的语法结构。它用来强调动作的承受者而不是执行者。

### 主动语态 vs 被动语态

| 对比 | 主动语态 | 被动语态 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 重点 | 动作的执行者 | 动作的承受者 |
| 结构 | 主语 + 动词 + 宾语 | 主语 + be + 过去分词 + by + 执行者 |
| 例句 | Tom called for help. | Help was called for by Tom. |
| 例句 | Music helps people relax. | People are helped to relax by music. |

---

## 被动语态的形成规则

### 基本结构
**主语 + be + 过去分词 (+ by + 执行者)**

### 各种时态的被动语态

#### 1. 一般现在时的被动语态
**am/is/are + 过去分词**

- 肯定式: The song is loved by many people.
- 否定式: The song is not loved by many people.
- 疑问式: Is the song loved by many people?

#### 2. 一般过去时的被动语态
**was/were + 过去分词**

- 肯定式: First aid was given to Mrs Bell.
- 否定式: First aid was not given to Mrs Bell.
- 疑问式: Was first aid given to Mrs Bell?

#### 3. 一般将来时的被动语态
**will be + 过去分词**

- 肯定式: The trees will be protected.
- 否定式: The trees will not be protected.
- 疑问式: Will the trees be protected?

#### 4. 现在进行时的被动语态
**am/is/are + being + 过去分词**

- 肯定式: The song is being played on the radio.
- 否定式: The song is not being played.
- 疑问式: Is the song being played?

#### 5. 过去进行时的被动语态
**was/were + being + 过去分词**

- 肯定式: The song was being played when I arrived.
- 否定式: The song was not being played.
- 疑问式: Was the song being played?

#### 6. 现在完成时的被动语态
**have/has + been + 过去分词**

- 肯定式: The song has been played many times.
- 否定式: The song has not been played.
- 疑问式: Has the song been played?

## 何时使用被动语态

### 1. 强调动作的承受者
- The song is loved by many people. (强调歌曲被喜爱)
- First aid was given to Mrs Bell. (强调急救被给予)

### 2. 执行者不重要或不知道
- The song is widely used in sports events. (不需要说明谁使用)
- Mistakes can be corrected. (执行者不重要)

### 3. 执行者是众所周知的
- The law protects wild animals. → Wild animals are protected by law.
- (执行者"法律"是众所周知的)

### 4. 科学或学术文章
- The experiment was conducted carefully.
- The results were analyzed.

## 主动语态改为被动语态的步骤

### 步骤 1：确定主语、动词和宾语
- 主动语态: Tom called for help.
- 主语: Tom
- 动词: called
- 宾语: help

### 步骤 2：将宾语变为被动语态的主语
- 新主语: help

### 步骤 3：将动词改为 be + 过去分词
- 原动词: called
- 过去分词: called
- 被动形式: was called

### 步骤 4：将原主语放在 by 后面（可选）
- 被动语态: Help was called for by Tom.
- 或简化为: Help was called for.

### 例子

**例子 1**
- 主动: Music helps people relax.
- 被动: People are helped to relax by music.

**例子 2**
- 主动: The teacher gave us homework.
- 被动: We were given homework by the teacher.

**例子 3**
- 主动: They are building a new school.
- 被动: A new school is being built.

## 被动语态的练习题

### 练习 1：改写为被动语态

1. Many people love this song.
   → This song **is loved** by many people.

2. Tom called for help.
   → Help **was called** for by Tom.

3. The teacher gave us homework.
   → We **were given** homework by the teacher.

4. They are building a new school.
   → A new school **is being built**.

5. People use trees to make paper.
   → Trees **are used** to make paper.

6. The law protects wild animals.
   → Wild animals **are protected** by the law.

7. First aid was given to Mrs Bell.
   → Mrs Bell **was given** first aid.

8. The forest is being destroyed.
   → People **are destroying** the forest.

### 练习 2：改写为主动语态

1. The song is loved by many people.
   → Many people **love** this song.

2. Help was called for by Tom.
   → Tom **called** for help.

3. We were given homework by the teacher.
   → The teacher **gave** us homework.

4. A new school is being built.
   → They **are building** a new school.

5. Trees are used to make paper.
   → People **use** trees to make paper.

6. Wild animals are protected by the law.
   → The law **protects** wild animals.

## 被动语态的特殊用法

### 1. 双宾语的被动语态

**主动**: The teacher gave us homework.
**被动**: 
- We were given homework by the teacher. (间接宾语变主语)
- Homework was given to us by the teacher. (直接宾语变主语)

**主动**: My mother bought me a book.
**被动**:
- I was bought a book by my mother.
- A book was bought for me by my mother.

### 2. 短语动词的被动语态

**主动**: They are building a new school.
**被动**: A new school is being built.

**主动**: People are cutting down the forest.
**被动**: The forest is being cut down.

### 3. 情态动词的被动语态

**主动**: You should tell the truth.
**被动**: The truth should be told.

**主动**: We must protect wild animals.
**被动**: Wild animals must be protected.

**主动**: They can solve this problem.
**被动**: This problem can be solved.

## 常见被动语态错误

### 错误 1：过去分词形式错误
❌ The song is love by many people.
✅ The song is loved by many people.

❌ First aid was give to Mrs Bell.
✅ First aid was given to Mrs Bell.

### 错误 2：be 动词形式错误
❌ The song are loved by many people.
✅ The song is loved by many people.

❌ Trees is used to make paper.
✅ Trees are used to make paper.

### 错误 3：时态错误
❌ The song is loved by many people yesterday.
✅ The song was loved by many people yesterday.

❌ First aid will given to Mrs Bell.
✅ First aid will be given to Mrs Bell.

### 错误 4：混淆主动和被动
❌ The forest is destroying by people.
✅ The forest is being destroyed by people.

❌ Many animals have endangered by pollution.
✅ Many animals have been endangered by pollution.

## 被动语态学习建议

### 1. 理解被动语态的概念
- 被动语态强调动作的承受者
- 被动语态由 be + 过去分词组成

### 2. 掌握各种时态的被动语态
- 学习每种时态的被动形式
- 理解时态在被动语态中的应用

### 3. 学习七年级下册的例句
- 从课文中找出被动语态的例子
- 理解被动语态在实际语境中的使用

### 4. 做大量练习题
- 改写主动语态为被动语态
- 改写被动语态为主动语态
- 完成句子练习

### 5. 在写作中应用
- 在作文中使用被动语态
- 检查被动语态的正确性
- 选择合适的语态

## 总结

### 关键要点
1. **被动语态的结构**: be + 过去分词
2. **各种时态的被动形式**: 根据时态改变 be 的形式
3. **何时使用被动语态**: 强调动作的承受者或执行者不重要
4. **主动改被动**: 将宾语变为主语，动词改为 be + 过去分词

### 学习建议
- 每天学习一个时态的被动语态
- 做练习题巩固
- 在阅读中识别被动语态
- 在写作中应用被动语态
- 定期复习和总结

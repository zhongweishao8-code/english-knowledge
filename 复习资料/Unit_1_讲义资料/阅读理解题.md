## Passage 1: The Blind Master of the Erhu

### 语法选择题 (Grammar Cloze)

**题目**: 选择最恰当的答案，每题只有一个正确答案。

| No. | 题目 | 选项 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | After his father died, Abing fell ill and sadly **___** his sight. | A. loses<br>B. lost<br>C. has lost | **B. lost** | 与前文 "fell ill" 时态一致，用过去式 |
| 2 | To support himself, he had to perform on the streets **___** money. | A. make<br>B. making<br>C. to make | **B. making** | "to perform...for money" 中，"for" 后跟动名词 |
| 3 | His life became even **___** as he grew older. | A. hard<br>B. harder<br>C. hardest | **B. harder** | "even" 修饰比较级，表示"更加" |
| 4 | He is the musician **___** composed the masterpiece. | A. who<br>B. which<br>C. what | **A. who** | 先行词是人 "musician"，用关系代词 "who" |
| 5 | The Moon Reflected on the **___** Spring. | A. Two<br>B. Second<br>C. Twice | **B. Second** | 序数词表示"第二"，用 "Second" |
| 6 | **___** to his music allows people to feel his deep emotions. | A. Listen<br>B. Listens<br>C. Listening | **C. Listening** | 动名词作主语，谓语用单数 |
| 7 | His most famous works **___** by them with a wire recorder. | A. recorded<br>B. were recorded<br>C. record | **B. were recorded** | 被动语态，过去式 |
| 8 | It is said **___** Abing wrote hundreds of pieces. | A. that<br>B. if<br>C. who | **A. that** | "It is said that..." 固定句型 |
| 9 | **___** Abing passed away many years ago, his spirit lives on. | A. Because<br>B. If<br>C. Although | **C. Although** | 表示转折关系，用 "Although" |
| 10 | Today, he is considered **___** amazing artist who turned his suffering into beautiful art. | A. a<br>B. an<br>C. the | **B. an** | "amazing" 以元音音素开头，用 "an" |

### 理解题

**题目 1**: What was Abing's real name?
**答案**: His real name was Hua Yanjun.

**题目 2**: Why did Abing become blind?
**答案**: After his father died, Abing fell ill and sadly lost his sight.

**题目 3**: What is Abing's most famous work?
**答案**: The Moon Reflected on the Second Spring (Erquan Yingyue).

**题目 4**: How many of Abing's pieces were saved for the world to hear?
**答案**: Only six were saved for the world to hear.

**题目 5**: What does the passage tell us about Abing?
**答案**: Despite his hardship and blindness, Abing continued to create beautiful music and is considered an amazing artist who turned his suffering into art.

## Passage 2: The Return of a Legend

### 语法选择题 (Grammar Cloze)

| No. | 题目 | 选项 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | In his early years, he traveled to Xinjiang alone. He wanted **___** for musical inspiration in the vast desert. | A. to look<br>B. looked<br>C. looking | **A. to look** | "want to do sth." 固定搭配 |
| 2 | His rough and husky voice **___** all over the country. | A. heard<br>B. was heard<br>C. hears | **B. was heard** | 被动语态，过去式 |
| 3 | However, **___** becoming famous, he chose to disappear in the public eye. | A. after<br>B. before<br>C. if | **A. after** | 表示时间顺序，用 "after" |
| 4 | He enjoys **___** books and studying traditional culture at home. | A. read<br>B. to read<br>C. reading | **C. reading** | "enjoy doing sth." 固定搭配 |
| 5 | **___** he was away for many years, fans never forgot him. | A. Because<br>B. If<br>C. Although | **C. Although** | 表示转折关系，用 "Although" |
| 6 | The song, **___** is based on a story from Strange Stories from a Chinese Studio, discusses truth and ugliness in society. | A. who<br>B. which<br>C. where | **B. which** | 非限制性定语从句，先行词是 "song"，用 "which" |
| 7 | Many people were **___** by the deep meaning behind the lyrics. | A. amazed<br>B. amazing<br>C. to amaze | **A. amazed** | 被动语态，过去分词作表语 |
| 8 | Dao Lang proves that true music **___** by time. | A. is not stopped<br>B. did not stop<br>C. will not stop | **A. is not stopped** | 被动语态，一般现在时 |
| 9 | If you focus on creating good art, you **___** success in the end. | A. find<br>B. found<br>C. will find | **C. will find** | if 引导的条件句，主句用将来时 |
| 10 | He is truly **___** artist with a pure heart. | A. A<br>B. an<br>C. / | **B. an** | "artist" 以元音音素开头，用 "an" |

### 理解题

**题目 1**: What is Dao Lang's real name?
**答案**: His real name is Luo Lin.

**题目 2**: Where did Dao Lang travel to look for musical inspiration?
**答案**: He traveled to Xinjiang.

**题目 3**: When did Dao Lang's album "The First Snow of 2002" come out?
**答案**: In 2004.

**题目 4**: Why did Dao Lang disappear from the public eye after becoming famous?
**答案**: He prefers a quiet life and enjoys reading books and studying traditional culture at home rather than attending TV shows.

**题目 5**: What does the passage tell us about Dao Lang's return?
**答案**: In 2023, Dao Lang returned with a new album "ShanGeLiaoZai". The song "Luocha Haishi" became a huge hit immediately. It proves that true music is not stopped by time, and if you focus on creating good art, you will find success in the end.

## Passage 3: Why Is "Classic Music" Classic?

### 语法选择题 (Grammar Cloze)

| No. | 题目 | 选项 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | Have you ever wondered **___** it has lasted for centuries? | A. what<br>B. why<br>C. that | **B. why** | 特殊疑问句，询问原因 |
| 2 | They can express feelings **___** are too deep for words. | A. who<br>B. what<br>C. that | **C. that** | 定语从句，先行词是 "feelings"，用 "that" |
| 3 | Listening to a symphony is like **___** a novel without letters. | A. to read<br>B. read<br>C. reading | **C. reading** | "like doing sth." 表示"像...一样" |
| 4 | These masterpieces **___** with strict rules and logic long ago. | A. were composed<br>B. are composed<br>C. composed | **A. were composed** | 被动语态，过去式 |
| 5 | Musicians used math and structure **___** a perfect world of sound. | A. build<br>B. to build<br>C. building | **B. to build** | "use...to do sth." 表示"用...来做..." |
| 6 | The exciting background music **___** during a hero's battle is usually orchestral. | A. playing<br>B. played<br>C. to play | **B. played** | 过去分词作定语，修饰 "music" |
| 7 | **___** you take away the music, the movie becomes much less interesting. | A. Unless<br>B. Although<br>C. If | **C. If** | 条件句，表示"如果...就..." |
| 8 | It is usually **___** than modern tunes because it has layers of meaning. | A. complex<br>B. more complex<br>C. the most complex | **B. more complex** | 比较级，与 "modern tunes" 比较 |
| 9 | It offers **___** special to anyone who is willing to listen. | A. nothing<br>B. something<br>C. anything | **B. something** | 肯定句中用 "something" |
| 10 | In history, neither time nor language **___** able to stop its beauty. | A. is<br>B. are<br>C. be | **B. are** | "neither...nor..." 就近原则，与 "language" 一致（复数） |

### 理解题

**题目 1**: Why do some students think classical music is boring?
**答案**: Some students might think classical music is boring or only for the elderly.

**题目 2**: How does classical music express feelings?
**答案**: Unlike pop songs, classical pieces often have no lyrics to tell you what to think. Yet, they can express feelings that are too deep for words. Listening to a symphony is like reading a novel without letters, and you create the story in your own mind.

**题目 3**: What makes classical music intellectually amazing?
**答案**: These masterpieces were composed with strict rules and logic. Musicians used math and structure to build a perfect world of sound. Every note has its own place.

**题目 4**: Where can we hear classical music in modern times?
**答案**: Classical music often appears in movies. The exciting background music playing during a hero's battle or a space adventure is usually orchestral.

**题目 5**: Why does classical music stand the test of time?
**答案**: Classical music is usually more complex than modern tunes because it has layers of meaning. It offers something special to anyone who is willing to listen. In history, neither time nor language has been able to stop its beauty.

## Passage 1: The Blind Master of the Erhu

### 文章标题
**The Blind Master of the Erhu** (二胡大师)

### 完整文本

Abing, whose real name was Hua Yanjun, is a legend in Chinese music history. He was born in Wuxi in 1893. His father was a Taoist priest(道士) who was excellent at music. Under his father's guidance, Abing learned to play many instruments when he was very young.

However, Abing's life was full of tragedy(悲剧). After his father died, Abing fell ill and sadly lost his sight. He became blind and homeless. To support himself, he had to perform on the streets for money. His life became even harder as he grew older. He often walked in the dark streets, wearing ragged clothes and carrying his erhu.

Despite the hardship, Abing continued to create music. He is the musician who composed the masterpiece, The Moon Reflected on the Second Spring(Erquan Yingyue). It is a melody full of sorrow and beauty. Listening to his music allows people to feel his deep emotions.

In 1950, two professors visited Abing. His most famous works were recorded by them with a wire recorder. It is said that Abing wrote hundreds of pieces, but only six were saved for the world to hear.

Although Abing passed away many years ago, his spirit lives on. Today, he is considered an amazing artist who turned his suffering into beautiful art.

### 文化背景

**阿炳 (Abing, 1893-1950)**
- 中国民族音乐家
- 二胡演奏大师
- 代表作: 《二泉映月》(The Moon Reflected on the Second Spring)
- 生平: 虽然失明且生活困难，但创作了许多经典作品
- 影响: 对中国民族音乐的发展做出了重大贡献

**二胡 (Erhu)**
- 中国传统弦乐器
- 有 2000 多年的历史
- 音色优美，表现力强
- 被誉为"中国的小提琴"

## Passage 2: The Return of a Legend

### 文章标题
**The Return of a Legend** (传奇回归)

### 完整文本

Dao Lang is a unique figure in the Chinese music industry. His real name is Luo Lin. Unlike many idols who love the spotlight, Dao Lang prefers a quiet life. In his early years, he traveled to Xinjiang alone. He wanted to look for musical inspiration in the vast desert. The local culture there deeply influenced his style.

In 2004, his album The First Snow of 2002 came out. It was a sudden success. His rough and husky voice was heard all over the country. At that time, whether in taxis or shops, you could hear his songs. It seemed that everyone knew his name overnight. However, after becoming famous, he chose to disappear in the public eye. He enjoys reading books and studying traditional culture at home rather than attending TV shows.

Although he was away for many years, fans never forgot him. In 2023, Dao Lang returned with a new album ShanGeLiaoZai. The song Luocha Haishi became a huge hit immediately. The song, which is based on a story from Strange Stories from a Chinese Studio (Liaozhai), discusses truth and ugliness in society.

Many people were amazed by the deep meaning behind the lyrics. Dao Lang proves that true music is not stopped by time. If you focus on creating good art, you will find success in the end. He is truly an artist with a pure heart.

## Passage 3: Why Is "Classic Music" Classic?

### 文章标题
**Why Is "Classic Music" Classic?** (为什么古典音乐是经典)

### 完整文本

Music is often called a universal language. When we mention classical music, some students might think it is boring or only for the elderly. However, have you ever wondered why it has lasted for centuries? While pop stars come and go, Beethoven and Mozart are still remembered.

First, classical music connects with our emotions deeply. Unlike pop songs, classical pieces often have no lyrics to tell you what to think. Yet, they can express feelings that are too deep for words. Listening to a symphony is like reading a novel without letters. You create the story in your own mind.

Second, the music is intellectually amazing. These masterpieces were composed with strict rules and logic long ago. Musicians used math and structure to build a perfect world of sound. It is like a grand building. Every note has its own place.

Third, classical music is still alive around us. It often appears in movies. The exciting background music playing during a hero's battle or a space adventure is usually orchestral(管弦乐的). If you take away the music, the movie becomes much less interesting.

Finally, classical music stands the test of time. It is usually more complex than modern tunes because it has layers of meaning. It offers something special to anyone who is willing to listen. In history, neither time nor language has been able to stop its beauty.

### 文化背景

**古典音乐 (Classical Music)**
- 历史: 约 1750-1820 年间发展成熟
- 代表人物: 贝多芬 (Beethoven)、莫扎特 (Mozart)、巴赫 (Bach)
- 特点: 结构严谨、和声复杂、表现力强
- 影响: 对现代音乐有深远影响
- 现代应用: 电影配乐、广告音乐等

**为什么古典音乐是经典**
1. 情感表达深刻
2. 音乐结构复杂
3. 经久不衰
4. 应用广泛
5. 文化价值高

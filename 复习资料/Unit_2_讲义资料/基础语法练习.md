## 第一部分：完成句子 (Complete the sentences with the given words in their proper forms)

### 要求：用所给单词的适当形式完成句子，每空格限填 1-3 词

| No. | 句子 | 给定词 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|--------|------|------|
| 1 | He completed the homework ____ than anyone else and got the highest mark. | careful | **more carefully** | 用比较级修饰动词 completed，表示"比任何人都更仔细" |

### 3.2 比较级和最高级专项选择题 (27 题)

| No. | 题目 | 选项 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | ____ you are, ____ mistakes you'll make. | A. The more carefully; the less<br>B. The more careful; the fewer<br>C. The more careful; the less<br>D. The more carefully; the fewer | **B** | "越...越..."结构，第一空用形容词，第二空用 fewer（修饰可数名词 mistakes） |

| 5 | Some of students think water is ____ than fire. | A. more safe<br>B. much safer<br>C. much safe<br>D. much more safe | **B** | much 修饰比较级 safer |
| 6 | - It is so cold this winter.<br>- Yes, but it was ____ colder last winter, I remember. | A. ever<br>B. even<br>C. almost<br>D. already | **B** | even 修饰比较级，表示"甚至更冷" |
| 7 | The air in the forest is ____ than that in the factory. | A. more better<br>B. much better<br>C. more good<br>D. well | **B** | much 修饰比较级 better，不能用 more better |
| 8 | He was not good at English. But he is ____ better at English this year. | A. a little<br>B. a bit<br>C. a little bit<br>D. All of the above. | **D** | a little、a bit、a little bit 都可以修饰比较级 |
| 9 | It's ____ colder today than yesterday. | A. more<br>B. even<br>C. quite<br>D. very | **B** | even 修饰比较级，表示"甚至更冷" |
| 10 | I think his Mojito is ____ than his last song. | A. more better<br>B. the best<br>C. much better<br>D. more good | **C** | much 修饰比较级 better |
| 11 | Mary thinks that maths is ____ than English, but I think maths is as ____ as English. | A. easiest; easier<br>B. easier; easy<br>C. easier; easier<br>D. easy; easy | **B** | 第一空用比较级 easier，第二空用原级 easy（as...as 结构） |
| 12 | He is ____ than me. | A. tall<br>B. taller<br>C. short<br>D. strong | **B** | 用比较级 taller |
| 13 | Cat's eyes on the roads make drivers ____ than before. | A. safest<br>B. safe<br>C. safer<br>D. the safest | **C** | 用比较级 safer，表示"比以前更安全" |
| 14 | No design is perfect. We should always think of ____ one. | A. the best<br>B. better<br>C. the better<br>D. a better | **D** | 用比较级 better，表示"更好的设计" |
| 15 | Which city is ____ from Shanghai, Suzhou or Hangzhou? | A. far<br>B. farther<br>C. farthest<br>D. furthest | **B** | 两个城市比较，用比较级 farther |
| 16 | - Are you feeling ____ today?<br>- Yes, thank you. | A. good<br>B. health<br>C. better<br>D. bad | **C** | 用比较级 better，表示"感觉更好" |
| 17 | It is not so ____ as yesterday, so there are ____ people walking in the park today. | A. cold, fewer<br>B. warm, more<br>C. cold, more<br>D. warm, most | **A** | "不如...冷"，所以人更少，用 fewer |
| 18 | Mr. Sun and Mr. Wind decided to have a competition to find out who was ____. | A. strong<br>B. stronger<br>C. strongest<br>D. the strongest | **B** | 两个人比较，用比较级 stronger |
| 19 | If you want to care for the earth, you should do ____ good things. | A. one<br>B. more<br>C. much<br>D. little | **B** | 用 more，表示"更多的好事" |
| 20 | Bob is younger than ____ in his class. | A. any student<br>B. other student<br>C. any students<br>D. any other student | **D** | 用 any other student，表示"比班里其他任何学生都年轻" |
| 21 | Mary plays the violin better than ____ girl in her school. | A. the other<br>B. any other<br>C. the others<br>D. others | **B** | 用 any other girl，表示"比学校里其他任何女生都拉得好" |
| 22 | - How about the play at the Grand Theatre last night?<br>- I've never seen ____. | A. a better one<br>B. the better one<br>C. a best one<br>D. the best one | **A** | 用 a better one，表示"一个更好的" |
| 23 | - Coffee or tea, Frank?<br>- Coffee, please. To get relaxed, ____ is better than a cup of coffee. | A. nothing<br>B. anything<br>C. something<br>D. everything | **A** | 用 nothing，表示"没有什么比一杯咖啡更好" |
| 24 | One of the most famous ____ in China ____ Beijing. | A. place; is<br>B. places; are<br>C. place; are<br>D. places; is | **D** | "最著名的地方之一"用复数 places，谓语用单数 is |
| 25 | Harbin became one of ____ tourist resorts in China last winter holidays. | A. popular<br>B. more popular<br>C. less popular<br>D. the most popular | **D** | 用最高级 the most popular，表示"最受欢迎的旅游胜地之一" |

| 27 | Nanjing isn't as large ____ Shanghai. However, it's the second ____ city in East China. | A. as; largest<br>B. as; larger<br>C. like; largest<br>D. like; large | **A** | "不如...大"用 as...as，"第二大城市"用最高级 largest |

| No. | 题目 | 选项 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|------|------|

| 4 | John and Robert didn't win the school bike race, ____ they smiled happily. | A. or<br>B. so<br>C. and<br>D. but | **D** | 表示转折，用 but |

| 9 | There are many trees and flowers on ____ sides of the road. | A. both<br>B. all<br>C. none<br>D. either | **A** | "道路的两边"用 both sides |
| 10 | Nothing happened to his smart camera ____ he tried again and again. | A. if<br>B. although<br>C. until<br>D. since | **C** | "直到...才..."用 until |
| 11 | It's great ____ the mountains. | A. to explore<br>B. explore<br>C. exploring<br>D. explored | **A** | It's great to do sth. 固定搭配 |
| 12 | They felt ____ when they heard the good news. | A. happy<br>B. happily<br>C. upset<br>D. well | **A** | feel 后用形容词作表语 |

| 15 | My daughter ____ any help. She can dress herself. | A. doesn't need<br>B. needn't<br>C. needs to<br>D. doesn't need to | **A** | 用 doesn't need，表示"不需要" |

## 第四部分：段落填空 (Choose the proper words in the box to complete the passage - 4 题)

### 题目：在方框中选择恰当的词填空，使短文通顺完整。(只填选项，每词限用一次)

**选项**: A. may  B. had better  C. needn't  D. mustn't

**短文**:
In some western countries, there are many traditional customs we should know. For example, when you visit someone, you 16 ____ give an even number (偶数) of flowers as a gift, because people think it 17 ____ bring bad luck. Also, you 18 ____ bring a very expensive gift. A small one is okay. At dinner, you 19 ____ start eating after the host starts. Understanding these customs can help us behave properly in Western social situations.

| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|------|------|------|
| 16 | **D. mustn't** | 表示"禁止"，不能送偶数朵花 |
| 17 | **A. may** | 表示"可能"，人们认为这可能会带来坏运气 |
| 18 | **B. had better** | 表示"最好不要"，不要送太贵的礼物 |
| 19 | **C. needn't** | 表示"不必"，不必等主人开始后才吃 |

## 学习建议

### 做题技巧
1. **先读题目**，理解题意
2. **分析选项**，排除明显错误
3. **应用规则**，选择正确答案
4. **检查答案**，确保无误

### 常见错误
- ❌ 比较级前加 the
- ❌ 最高级前不加 the
- ❌ 形容词比较级形式错误
- ❌ 修饰词使用不当
- ❌ 倍数表达错误

### 复习建议
- 掌握比较级和最高级的形成规则
- 理解 8 条比较级用法和 4 条最高级用法
- 做大量练习题，巩固知识
- 记录错题，分析错误原因
- 在写作中应用这些知识

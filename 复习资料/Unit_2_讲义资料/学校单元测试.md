**短文**:

Qinqiang Opera is one of the earliest forms of opera in China. It started from the ancient 16 ____ songs and dances of the people in Shaanxi and Gansu provinces.

Hou Yan started learning Qinqiang Opera at the age of 4, training in the basic skills of Qinqiang Opera is very 17 ____. While training, each basic move would have to be held accurately for half an hour and a single 18 ____ could mean she had to start all over again. Her hard work finally paid off. Hou became a performer in a troupe(剧团)in Yinchuan at 16. In 1993, the sweeping trend(趋势)of 19 ____ and Western music hit Qinqiang Opera hard. "As we become more 23 ____ about our culture, more young people feel proud of our traditional culture and take it as a fashionable trend. They also become promoters of our own culture." said Hou.

### 答案和解析

### 短文翻译

秦腔是中国最早的戏曲形式之一。它起源于陕西和甘肃人民的古代民间歌曲和舞蹈。

侯燕从 4 岁开始学习秦腔，秦腔基本功的训练非常有挑战性。在训练中，每个基本动作都必须准确地保持半小时，一个错误就意味着她必须重新开始。她的辛勤工作最终得到了回报。侯燕在 16 岁时成为银川一个剧团的演员。1993 年，流行音乐和西方音乐的浪潮对秦腔造成了沉重打击。剧团关闭了，侯燕不得不离开歌剧舞台。

然而，在过去的几十年里，中国做出了巨大努力来发展其传统文化。侯燕在 2000 年回到舞台，并全身心投入到秦腔中。

现在，侯燕的团队每年进行数百场演出。侯燕歌剧团的平均年龄是 30 岁。2021 年，他们提供了约 150 场学校演出。

"随着我们对自己文化更有信心，越来越多的年轻人为我们的传统文化感到骄傲，并将其视为一种时尚潮流。他们也成为了我们自己文化的推广者。"侯燕说。

### 4.2.1 语法选择 (5 题)

**题目**: Community Garden Project

Our class organized a project to clean up an old garden. There's some rubbish in the garden. The goal was to 8 ____ and plant flowers. Some students wore gloves to pick up bottles, while others dug holes.

At first, we argued about the plan. Tom suggested 9 ____ more tools, but Emma said, "We have 10 ____ time to prepare. Let's start now!" Finally, we agreed. While working, an old lady passed by and said, "You kids seem 11 ____! This garden looks much cleaner than before!" Her words encouraged us to work harder.

By noon, we had collected a few bags of rubbish. It was really tiring. 12 ____ we felt proud of ourselves.

| No. | 题目 | 选项 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|------|------|
| 8 | The goal was to ____ and plant flowers. | A. pick up it<br>B. pick it up<br>C. pick them up | **C** | 代词 them 放在 pick 和 up 之间 |
| 9 | Tom suggested ____ more tools. | A. to buy<br>B. buying<br>C. buy | **B** | suggest doing sth. 固定搭配 |
| 10 | We have ____ time to prepare. | A. a little<br>B. few<br>C. little | **C** | little 修饰不可数名词 time，表示"很少" |
| 11 | You kids seem ____! | A. amazing<br>B. amazed<br>C. amaze | **A** | seem 后用形容词，amazing 表示"令人惊叹的" |
| 12 | ____ we felt proud of ourselves. | A. but<br>B. and<br>C. so | **A** | 表示转折，虽然累但感到骄傲 |

### 4.2.2 语法填空 (8 题)

**题目**: An 21 ____ (forget) school trip

Last week, our class visited a local museum to learn about cultural traditions. The guide showed us ancient tools and explained how people used to 22 ____ (communication) through symbols. I was surprised to 23 ____ (real) how creative they were!

During the tour, my friend Tom accidentally knocked over a display. He turned pale and said, "This is so 24 ____ (embarrass)!" The guide smiled and replied, "Don't worry. Just 25 ____ (proper) explain what happened." Tom gave a reasonable explanation and 26 ____ (apology) sincerely to the museum staff(工作人员), and everyone understood it was an accident.

In the afternoon, we attended a workshop on music. The teacher taught us the 27 ____ (pronounce) of some traditional songs. Though I wasn't 28 ____ (confidence) at first, I finally sang along with the class. The trip was not only educational but also filled with joy!

| No. | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|
| 21 | **unforgettable** | 形容词，修饰 school trip |
| 22 | **communicate** | 动词原形，used to do sth. 固定搭配 |
| 23 | **realize** | 动词原形，to realize how creative |
| 24 | **embarrassing** | 形容词，表示"令人尴尬的" |
| 25 | **properly** | 副词，修饰 explain |
| 26 | **apologized** | 动词过去式，与前文时态一致 |
| 27 | **pronunciation** | 名词，表示"发音" |
| 28 | **confident** | 形容词，wasn't confident |

### 4.2.3 长文章阅读 (10 题)

**题目**: Language is not the only way to communicate. Sometimes, a simple gesture can be 1 ____ than a thousand words. This is a lesson I learned during my trip to Italy last year.

On the 2 ____ day of my trip, I visited a small, beautiful village. While taking photos, I lost my way. I felt anxious until I saw an elderly woman 3 ____ on a bench by the river. I walked up to her, hoping to ask for directions to the train station. "Excuse me, could you help me?" I asked in English. The woman looked at me with a confused smile. It was clear that English 4 ____ by her. We stood there for a moment in silence. I didn't know 5 ____ I should do next. Suddenly, an idea came to my mind. I decided 6 ____ my hands instead of my mouth. I made a "choo-choo" sound and moved my arms like wheels. The woman's eyes lit up immediately! She pointed down the street and turned her hand to the left.

7 ____ we spoke different languages, we understood each other perfectly. Using body language effectively is truly a useful skill for travelers. 8 ____ her directions, I found the station easily. It was a bridge 9 ____ connected two strangers. Seldom 10 ____ I forget that warm afternoon. It proved that kindness expressed through gestures is a universal language.

| No. | 题目 | 选项 | 答案 | 解析 |
|-----|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | a simple gesture can be ____ than a thousand words. | A. effective<br>B. more effective<br>C. most effective | **B** | 比较级，表示"比一千句话更有效" |
| 2 | On the ____ day of my trip. | A. two<br>B. second<br>C. twice | **B** | 序数词，表示"第二天" |
| 3 | I saw an elderly woman ____ on a bench. | A. sitting<br>B. sat<br>C. to sit | **A** | 现在分词作定语 |
| 4 | It was clear that English ____ by her. | A. doesn't speak<br>B. was not spoken<br>C. didn't speak | **B** | 被动语态，过去式 |
| 5 | I didn't know ____ I should do next. | A. what<br>B. that<br>C. which | **A** | 特殊疑问词，表示"做什么" |
| 6 | I decided ____ my hands instead of my mouth. | A. use<br>B. using<br>C. to use | **C** | decide to do sth. 固定搭配 |
| 7 | ____ we spoke different languages, we understood each other perfectly. | A. Because<br>B. If<br>C. Although | **C** | 表示转折，虽然说不同的语言 |
| 8 | ____ her directions, I found the station easily. | A. To follow<br>B. Following<br>C. Followed | **B** | 现在分词作状语 |
| 9 | It was a bridge ____ connected two strangers. | A. who<br>B. that<br>C. what | **B** | 定语从句，先行词是 bridge |
| 10 | Seldom ____ I forget that warm afternoon. | A. do<br>B. did<br>C. will | **A** | 倒装句，Seldom do I forget |

### 长文章翻译

语言不是唯一的交流方式。有时，一个简单的手势比千言万语更有效。这是我去年意大利之旅中学到的一课。

在我旅行的第二天，我访问了一个小而美丽的村庄。在拍照时，我迷路了。我感到焦虑，直到我看到一位老妇人坐在河边的长凳上。我走向她，希望能问到去火车站的路。"对不起，你能帮我吗？"我用英语问。那位妇女用困惑的微笑看着我。很明显，她不会说英语。我们沉默地站了一会儿。我不知道接下来该做什么。突然，一个想法出现在我的脑海中。我决定用我的手而不是嘴巴。我发出"呜呜"的声音，挥动我的手臂像车轮。那位妇女的眼睛立刻亮了起来！她指向街道，转过手向左。

虽然我们说不同的语言，但我们完全理解彼此。有效地使用肢体语言确实是旅行者的一项有用技能。按照她的指示，我轻松地找到了火车站。这是一座连接两个陌生人的桥梁。我很少忘记那个温暖的下午。它证明了通过手势表达的善良是一种通用语言。

## 学习建议

### 做题技巧
1. **段落填空**: 先读全文，理解大意，再选择合适的词
2. **语法选择**: 分析句子结构，应用语法规则
3. **语法填空**: 根据上下文和语法规则填空
4. **阅读理解**: 快速阅读，查找关键信息

### 常见错误
- ❌ 不理解短文的整体意思
- ❌ 忽视语法规则
- ❌ 词形转换错误
- ❌ 时态不一致

### 复习建议
- 理解每道题的解析
- 记录常见错误
- 做相似的练习题
- 在实际应用中巩固知识

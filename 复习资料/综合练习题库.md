## 第一部分：语法练习题

### 一、时态练习

#### 练习 1：一般现在时

1. He **________** (like) music very much.
   **答案**: likes

2. They **________** (not/communicate) well.
   **答案**: don't communicate

3. **________** you **________** (prefer) texting or calling?
   **答案**: Do, prefer

4. She **________** (play) the piano every day.
   **答案**: plays

5. The song **________** (represent) Chinese culture.
   **答案**: represents

#### 练习 2：一般过去时

1. Tom **________** (call) for help yesterday.
   **答案**: called

2. The ambulance **________** (arrive) quickly.
   **答案**: arrived

3. She **________** (not/tell) the truth.
   **答案**: didn't tell

4. **________** you **________** (see) the movie?
   **答案**: Did, see

5. He **________** (go) to the hospital last week.
   **答案**: went

#### 练习 3：现在进行时

1. I **________** (text) my friends right now.
   **答案**: am texting

2. She **________** (play) the piano at the moment.
   **答案**: is playing

3. They **________** (not/communicate) well.
   **答案**: aren't communicating

4. **________** you **________** (listen) to music?
   **答案**: Are, listening

5. The birds **________** (fly) in the sky.
   **答案**: are flying

#### 练习 4：过去进行时

1. The swan **________** (sit) very close.
   **答案**: was sitting

2. The baby **________** (climb) through the feathers.
   **答案**: was climbing

3. They **________** (not/listen) to the teacher.
   **答案**: weren't listening

4. **________** you **________** (do) when I called?
   **答案**: Were, doing

5. Sam **________** (watch) the swans while his father **________** (prepare) dinner.
   **答案**: was watching, was preparing

### 二、被动语态练习

#### 练习 1：改写为被动语态

1. Many people love this song.
   → This song **is loved** by many people.

2. Tom called for help.
   → Help **was called** for by Tom.

3. The teacher gave us homework.
   → We **were given** homework by the teacher.

4. They are building a new school.
   → A new school **is being built**.

5. People use trees to make paper.
   → Trees **are used** to make paper.

#### 练习 2：完成句子

1. The song **________** (love) by many people.
   **答案**: is loved

2. First aid **________** (give) to Mrs Bell.
   **答案**: was given

3. Trees **________** (use) to make paper.
   **答案**: are used

4. The forest **________** (destroy) by people.
   **答案**: is being destroyed

5. Many animals **________** (endanger) by pollution.
   **答案**: have been endangered

### 三、从句练习

#### 练习 1：定语从句

1. The girl **who** likes music is my friend.
   (用 who 连接)

2. The song **that** I like is beautiful.
   (用 that 连接)

3. The book **which** I read yesterday is interesting.
   (用 which 连接)

4. The boy **whose** father is a musician is Tom.
   (用 whose 连接)

#### 练习 2：状语从句

1. **While** the mother swan was sitting, the baby was climbing.
   (用 while 连接)

2. **After** the ambulance arrived, Mrs Bell received first aid.
   (用 after 连接)

3. I like music **because** it helps me relax.
   (用 because 连接)

4. **If** you study hard, you will pass the exam.
   (用 if 连接)

5. **Although** he was shocked, he stayed calm.
   (用 although 连接)

## 第二部分：词汇练习题

### 一、词形转换

#### 练习 1：名词转形容词

1. beauty → **beautiful**
2. confidence → **confident**
3. silence → **silent**
4. kindness → **kind**

#### 练习 2：形容词转副词

1. beautiful → **beautifully**
2. calm → **calmly**
3. smooth → **smoothly**
4. quick → **quickly**

#### 练习 3：动词转名词

1. communicate → **communication**
2. protect → **protection**
3. encourage → **encouragement**
4. describe → **description**

### 二、同义词替换

1. I like music. → I **enjoy** music.
2. The song is beautiful. → The song is **lovely**.
3. He is famous. → He is **well-known**.
4. We help each other. → We **support** each other.
5. I am sorry. → I **apologise**.

### 三、反义词替换

1. I like music. → I **dislike** music.
2. The song is beautiful. → The song is **ugly**.
3. He is famous. → He is **unknown**.
4. She is happy. → She is **sad**.
5. The music is loud. → The music is **quiet**.

### 四、词根词缀

1. music + -ian = **musician**
2. beauty + -ful = **beautiful**
3. popular + -ity = **popularity**
4. un- + known = **unknown**
5. prefer + -ence = **preference**

## 第三部分：阅读理解题

### 阅读文章 1：Music and Life

Music is an important part of our lives. It can help us relax, improve our mood, and even help us learn. Many people enjoy listening to different kinds of music, such as pop, rock, classical, and folk music.

Beethoven was a famous composer who wrote many beautiful symphonies. His Symphony No. 5 is one of the best-known works in the classical music world. The melody is very powerful and inspiring. Every time you listen to it, you feel energetic and brave.

Music can also help us communicate with others. Through music, we can express our feelings and emotions. It brings people together and creates a sense of unity.

**问题**:
1. What can music help us do?
   **答案**: Music can help us relax, improve our mood, and help us learn.

2. Who was Beethoven?
   **答案**: Beethoven was a famous composer.

3. What is Symphony No. 5?
   **答案**: It is one of Beethoven's best-known works.

4. How does music help us communicate?
   **答案**: Through music, we can express our feelings and emotions.

### 阅读文章 2：Helping Others

Tom is a 13-year-old boy who loves helping others. One day, he saw an old woman who had fallen on the street. Without hesitation, he called for help and stayed with her until the ambulance arrived. The paramedics gave her first aid and took her to the hospital.

The old woman was very grateful for Tom's help. She said that Tom's kindness saved her life. Tom felt happy that he could help someone in need. He realized that helping others is not only good for them, but also makes us feel good about ourselves.

**问题**:
1. What did Tom do when he saw the old woman?
   **答案**: He called for help and stayed with her.

2. What did the paramedics do?
   **答案**: They gave her first aid and took her to the hospital.

3. How did the old woman feel about Tom's help?
   **答案**: She was very grateful and said his kindness saved her life.

4. What did Tom realize?
   **答案**: He realized that helping others is good for them and makes us feel good about ourselves.

## 第四部分：写作练习题

### 练习 1：句子改写

1. 改写为被动语态：
   - I like music. → Music **is liked** by me.
   - She plays the piano. → The piano **is played** by her.

2. 改写为复合句：
   - I like music. It helps me relax. → I like music **because** it helps me relax.
   - The swan was sitting. The baby was climbing. → **While** the swan was sitting, the baby was climbing.

### 练习 2：段落写作

**题目**: Write about your favorite music and why you like it.

**范文**:
My favorite music is classical music. I like it because it is beautiful and relaxing. When I listen to classical music, I feel calm and peaceful. Classical music can help me concentrate on my studies. I also enjoy listening to pop music, but I prefer classical music. I think music is an important part of our lives, and everyone should enjoy music.

**要求**:
- 使用至少 3 个不同的时态
- 使用至少 2 个从句
- 使用至少 5 个形容词

### 练习 3：对话写作

**题目**: Write a dialogue between two friends about communication.

**范文**:
A: Hi, how do you usually communicate with your friends?
B: I usually text them or chat with them on social media.
A: Do you prefer texting or calling?
B: I prefer texting because it's more convenient. But sometimes I call them when I need to talk about something important.
A: I agree. Different situations require different ways of communication.
B: Yes, and we should be careful about how we communicate to avoid misunderstandings.

## 第五部分：综合测试题

### 测试 1：Unit 1-2 综合测试

#### 第一部分：选择题

1. He **________** music very much.
   A. like  B. likes  C. liked  D. is liking
   **答案**: B

2. The song **________** by many people.
   A. is loved  B. loves  C. loved  D. is loving
   **答案**: A

3. **________** you **________** texting or calling?
   A. Do, prefer  B. Does, prefer  C. Did, prefer  D. Are, preferring
   **答案**: A

4. The girl **who** likes music **________** my friend.
   A. is  B. are  C. was  D. were
   **答案**: A

5. **While** I was reading, my brother **________** playing.
   A. was  B. is  C. were  D. are
   **答案**: A

#### 第二部分：填空题

1. The **________** (beautiful) of the melody is amazing.
   **答案**: beauty

2. She **________** (communicate) with her friends every day.
   **答案**: communicates

3. The song **________** (love) by many people.
   **答案**: is loved

4. I **________** (prefer) classical music to pop music.
   **答案**: prefer

5. **________** (although) he was tired, he continued working.
   **答案**: Although

#### 第三部分：改写句子

1. I like music. (改为否定句)
   → I **don't like** music.

2. She plays the piano. (改为被动语态)
   → The piano **is played** by her.

3. The song is beautiful. (改为反义词)
   → The song is **ugly**.

4. I like music. It helps me relax. (合并为一句)
   → I like music **because** it helps me relax.

5. He is honest. I know this. (合并为一句)
   → I know **that he is honest**.

## 学习建议

### 1. 系统地做练习题
- 按照语法、词汇、阅读、写作的顺序做题
- 每天做 10-15 道题

### 2. 检查答案并分析错误
- 做完题后立即检查答案
- 分析错误原因
- 记录常见错误

### 3. 定期复习
- 每周复习一次
- 重点复习错题
- 总结规律

### 4. 在实际应用中练习
- 在阅读中应用所学语法
- 在写作中应用所学词汇
- 在对话中应用所学表达

- 计时做题
- 评估学习效果

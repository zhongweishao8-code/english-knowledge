## 词根词缀分析

### 1. music → musician, musical, musically

**词根**: music (音乐)

**词缀分析**:
- **musician** = music + -ian (表示人)
- **musical** = music + -al (表示形容词)
- **musically** = musical + -ly (表示副词)

**含义**:
- music (n.) - 音乐
- musician (n.) - 音乐家
- musical (adj.) - 音乐的；音乐剧
- musically (adv.) - 音乐地

**例句**:
- I love **music**. (我喜欢音乐)
- She is a talented **musician**. (她是个有才华的音乐家)
- She has a **musical** talent. (她有音乐天赋)
- She sings **musically**. (她唱得很有音乐感)

## A2 听力练习：音乐介绍

### 听力文本

**Introduction 1: Jasmine Flower**

This is a traditional Chinese folk song called Jasmine Flower. It's an unknown composer's work, but it has become very well-known both in China and abroad. The song mainly tells about the beauty of the jasmine flower. The melody is very beautiful and relaxing. Many people love this song because it represents Chinese culture and tradition.

**Introduction 2: Symphony No. 5**

This is Beethoven's Symphony No. 5, one of the best-known works in the classical music world. Beethoven was a great composer from Austria. This symphony is about the fight against difficulties in life. The music is very powerful and inspiring. Every time you listen to it, you feel energetic and brave.

## B2 对话听力：音乐表演邀请

### 听力文本

> **Bailing**: Hi, Mary! There are some great music events this weekend. Would you like to go with me?
> **Mary**: Yes, I'd love to. Which one do you have in mind?
> **Bailing**: Let's check out the website for the events.
> **Mary**: Here are three shows. They all look interesting. It's hard to choose!
> **Bailing**: Shall we watch Journey to the West? I haven't seen it done in English before. Maybe it's new to you too.
> **Mary**: Actually, I've seen it already. How about Sunflower Chorus? They have sweet voices and it's a charity show. They are raising money for homeless animals.
> **Bailing**: Good choice. Let's watch Sunflower Chorus's Charity Show this weekend.
> **Mary**: That'll be great!

### 听力理解

**问题 1**: Which show(s) would they like to watch?
**答案**: They would like to watch Sunflower Chorus's Charity Show.

**问题 2**: Why did Mary choose this show?
**答案**: Because they have sweet voices and it's a charity show. They are raising money for homeless animals.

**问题 3**: What did Bailing suggest first?
**答案**: Bailing suggested watching Journey to the West.

## 听力技巧

### 1. 听力前的准备
- 预读题目和选项
- 了解听力内容的主题
- 预测可能出现的关键词

### 2. 听力中的技巧
- 集中注意力，不要分心
- 记下关键信息
- 注意说话人的语调和重音

### 3. 听力后的检查
- 检查答案的完整性
- 确认理解的准确性
- 复习不懂的地方

## 关键词汇回顾

### 音乐相关词汇
- **folk music** - 民间音乐
- **classical music** - 古典音乐
- **melody** - 旋律
- **theme** - 主题
- **composer** - 作曲家
- **musician** - 音乐家

### 表达观点的词汇
- **I'd love to** - 我很乐意
- **It's hard to choose** - 很难选择
- **Good choice** - 好选择
- **That'll be great** - 那太好了

### 听力中常见的短语
- **have in mind** - 想到；考虑
- **check out** - 查看；借阅
- **raise money** - 筹集资金
- **charity show** - 慈善演出

## 扩展学习

2. Do you like to attend music concerts?
3. What makes a good musician?

- 邀请朋友参加活动

## 词转规律分类

**music → musician → musical → musically**

- musician (n.) - 音乐家
- musical (adj.) - 音乐的；音乐剧
- musically (adv.) - 音乐地

**beauty → beautiful → beautifully**
- beauty (n.) - 美；美丽
- beautiful (adj.) - 美丽的
- beautifully (adv.) - 美妙地

**smooth → smoothly**
- smooth (adj.) - 平滑的；流畅的
- smoothly (adv.) - 连续而流畅地

**calm → calmly**
- calm (adj.) - 沉着的

- calm (v.) - 使镇静

## 词转详解

### 1. music → musician → musical

**music (n.)** - 音乐
- 例句: I love listening to music.
- 用法: 不可数名词

**musician (n.)** - 音乐家
- 例句: Beethoven is a famous musician.
- 用法: 可数名词，表示从事音乐的人

**musical (adj.)** - 音乐的；音乐剧
- 例句: She has a musical talent.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词

---

### 2. beauty → beautiful → beautifully

**beauty (n.)** - 美；美丽
- 例句: The beauty of the flower is amazing.
- 用法: 不可数名词

**beautiful (adj.)** - 美丽的
- 例句: The melody is beautiful.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词或作表语

**beautifully (adv.)** - 美妙地
- 例句: She sings beautifully.
- 用法: 作副词修饰动词

---

### 3. popular → popularity

**popular (adj.)** - 流行的；受欢迎的
- 例句: Pop music is popular among teenagers.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词或作表语

**popularity (n.)** - 流行；受欢迎
- 例句: The popularity of this song is increasing.
- 用法: 不可数名词

---

### 4. prefer → preference

**prefer (v.)** - 更喜欢
- 例句: I prefer classical music to pop music.
- 用法: 动词，后接名词或动名词

**preference (n.)** - 偏爱；偏好
- 例句: What is your music preference?
- 用法: 可数名词

---

### 5. choose → choice

**choose (v.)** - 选择
- 例句: Which show do you choose?
- 用法: 动词，过去式为 chose，过去分词为 chosen

**choice (n.)** - 选择
- 例句: You have many choices of music.
- 用法: 可数名词

---

### 6. confidence → confident

**confidence (n.)** - 信心
- 例句: Music brought him confidence.
- 用法: 不可数名词

**confident (adj.)** - 自信的
- 例句: Kevin became more confident.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词或作表语

---

### 7. relax → relaxation / relaxing / relaxed

**relax (v.)** - 放松
- 例句: Music helps you relax.
- 用法: 动词

**relaxation (n.)** - 放松
- 例句: Music brings relaxation.
- 用法: 不可数名词

**relaxing (adj.)** - 令人放松的
- 例句: Classical music is relaxing.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词

**relaxed (adj.)** - 感到放松的
- 例句: He felt relaxed after listening to music.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词或作表语

---

### 8. encourage → encouragement / encouraging

**encourage (v.)** - 鼓励
- 例句: Music encouraged Kevin to sing.
- 用法: 动词，后接名词或不定式

**encouragement (n.)** - 鼓励
- 例句: He needed encouragement from his friends.
- 用法: 不可数名词

**encouraging (adj.)** - 令人鼓舞的
- 例句: The teacher gave encouraging words.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词

---

### 9. wide → widely / widen

**wide (adj.)** - 宽的
- 例句: The stage is wide.
- 用法: 作形容词修饰名词或作表语

**widely (adv.)** - 广泛地；普遍地
- 例句: The song is widely used in sports.
- 用法: 作副词修饰动词或形容词

**widen (v.)** - 拓宽
- 例句: Music can widen your horizons.
- 用法: 动词

## 学习建议

### 1. 学习方法
- **第一步**: 理解词根的含义
- **第二步**: 掌握词性转换的规律
- **第三步**: 记住每个词形的用法
- **第四步**: 在句子中应用

### 2. 常见规律
- **-tion / -sion**: 动词 → 名词 (relax → relaxation)
- **-ment**: 动词 → 名词 (encourage → encouragement)
- **-ful / -less**: 名词 → 形容词 (beauty → beautiful)
- **-ly**: 形容词 → 副词 (calm → calmly)
- **-ing / -ed**: 动词 → 形容词 (relax → relaxing / relaxed)

### 3. 复习建议
- 定期复习词转表
- 做练习题巩固
- 在阅读中注意词形变化
- 在写作中应用所学

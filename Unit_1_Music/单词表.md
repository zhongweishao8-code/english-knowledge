## 词汇学习重点

### 1. 复合形容词 (Compound Adjectives)
- **best-known** = a work that is best known (最知名的)
- **well-known** = widely known (众所周知的)
- **ten-minute walk** = a walk of ten minutes (十分钟的步行)
- **well-paid job** = a job that was paid well (薪水高的工作)
- **world-famous** = famous around the world (世界著名的)

### 2. 关键短语
- **come out freely** - 自由地表达
- **used to** - 过去常常
- **encourage sb. to do sth.** - 鼓励某人做某事
- **make fun of** - 嘲笑
- **not only...but also...** - 不仅...而且...
- **both...and...** - 既...又...

### 3. 词汇填空练习
Fill in the blanks with the proper forms of the words and phrases in the box.

*come out freely; used to; encourage; make fun of; confident*

Kevin is a 13-year-old boy from Manchester. He **used to** have a stutter and was very shy, so other kids **made fun of** him. Luckily, later Kevin found that his words could **come out freely** in the chorus. The music has brought him **confidence** after he learned singing so that he can give talks to other young people. He wants to tell the world what magic music can do.

## 音乐相关词汇扩展

### 音乐风格 (Music Styles)
- pop music - 流行音乐
- rock music - 摇滚乐
- classical music - 古典音乐
- folk music - 民间音乐
- jazz - 爵士乐
- country music - 乡村音乐

### 音乐元素 (Musical Elements)
- melody - 旋律
- rhythm - 节奏
- harmony - 和声
- lyrics - 歌词
- beat - 节拍
- tempo - 速度

### 音乐活动 (Musical Activities)
- sing - 唱歌
- play an instrument - 演奏乐器
- compose - 作曲
- perform - 表演
- conduct - 指挥
- record - 录音

## 句型分类学习

### 1. 询问和描述动物句型
- **What is the largest living animal on the Earth?** - 地球上最大的活动物是什么？
- **How does it look?** - 它看起来怎样？
- **Where does it live?** - 它住在哪里？
- **What does it eat?** - 它吃什么？

### 2. 表达关心和担忧句型
- **You look worried, Ala.** - 你看起来很担忧。
- **What's the problem?** - 问题是什么？
- **I'm sorry about that.** - 我为此感到遗憾。
- **Don't worry.** - 别担心。

### 3. 表达意图和计划句型
- **I'm leaving with my family in a few days.** - 我将在几天内和我的家人一起离开。
- **We are running out of food so we have to move.** - 我们食物快用完了，所以我们必须搬家。
- **We are going to set out for the north.** - 我们将出发去北方。
- **But we don't know what is waiting for us.** - 但我们不知道等待我们的是什么。

### 4. 过去进行时句型
- **The mother swan was sitting very close, not moving.** - 母天鹅坐得很近，不动。
- **Both birds were listening and waiting.** - 两只鸟都在听和等待。
- **A small head was pushing through the mother's feathers.** - 一个小头从母亲的羽毛中推出来。
- **The mother swan was watching them use their legs.** - 母天鹅在看它们使用腿。

### 5. 描述场景句型
- **It was still only a little after seven o'clock.** - 才刚过七点。
- **The Sun was pale, and the air was cool.** - 太阳很苍白，空气很凉爽。
- **The morning smelled delicious.** - 早晨闻起来很香。
- **Sam sat down quietly by the pool.** - 山姆静静地坐在池塘边。

## 练习题

### 练习 1：完成对话

1. A: **What is the largest living animal on the Earth?**
   B: **The blue whale.**

2. A: **You look worried, Ala. What's the problem?**
   B: **We are running out of food so we have to move.**

3. A: **Where are you leaving for?**
   B: **We are going to set out for the north.**

### 练习 2：造句

1. 询问动物：
   - Where does the giant panda live?

2. 表达关心：
   - I'm sorry about that. Don't worry.

3. 描述场景：
   - The morning smelled delicious. Sam sat down quietly by the pool.

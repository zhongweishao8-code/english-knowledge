## 语法主题：过去进行时 (Past Continuous)

### 定义
**过去进行时**表示在过去某个时间点或时间段内正在进行的动作。

### 形成规则

#### 肯定式
**结构**：was/were + -ing

| 主语 | 动词形式 | 补语 |
|------|---------|------|
| I/He/She/It | was watching | a bird on the phone wire at 4:30 p.m. yesterday. |
| We/You/They | were watching | a bird on the phone wire at 4:30 p.m. yesterday. |

#### 否定式
**结构**：was/were + not + -ing

| 主语 | 动词形式 | 补语 |
|------|---------|------|
| I/He/She/It | wasn't watching | a bird on the phone wire at 4:30 p.m. yesterday. |
| We/You/They | weren't watching | a bird on the phone wire at 4:30 p.m. yesterday. |

#### 一般疑问句
**结构**：Was/Were + 主语 + -ing?

| 问句 | 肯定回答 | 否定回答 |
|------|---------|---------|
| Was he/she watching a bird? | Yes, he/she was. | No, he/she wasn't. |
| Were you/they watching a bird? | Yes, we/they were. | No, we/they weren't. |

#### 特殊疑问句
**结构**：Wh-question + was/were + 主语 + -ing?

| 特殊疑问词 | 动词形式 | 主语 | 补语 |
|-----------|---------|------|------|
| What | were | you | doing at this time yesterday? |
| Who | was | swimming | in the pond? |
| How | was | the tiger | hunting for food? |

## 用法规则

### 用法 1：表示过去某个时间点正在进行的动作
**例句**：
- The mother swan **was sitting** very close, not moving.
  (母天鹅坐得很近，不动。)
- Both birds **were listening** and waiting.
  (两只鸟都在听和等待。)

### 用法 2：表示两个过去事件同时进行
**例句**：
- Sam **was watching** the swans, while his father **was preparing** dinner at the campsite.
  (山姆在看天鹅，而他的父亲在营地准备晚餐。)

### 用法 3：描述过去的情景或背景
**例句**：
- A small head **was pushing** through the mother's feathers.
  (一个小头正从母亲的羽毛中推出来。)

## 练习题

### 练习 1：完成句子

1. The mother swan **was sitting** (sit) very close.
2. Both birds **were listening** (listen) and waiting.
3. A small head **was pushing** (push) through the feathers.
4. The baby **was climbing** (climb) out of the nest.

### 练习 2：改写句子

1. Sam watched the swans. His father prepared dinner.
   → Sam **was watching** the swans while his father **was preparing** dinner.

2. The baby climbed out. The mother watched.
   → The baby **was climbing** out while the mother **was watching**.

### 练习 3：造句

1. 关于观察动物：
   - The birds **were watching** the baby swans.

2. 关于自然场景：
   - The sun **was shining** and the birds **were singing**.

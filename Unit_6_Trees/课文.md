- **Oak trees**: Strong and long-lived; provide shelter for animals
- **Pine trees**: Evergreen; used for timber and paper
- **Maple trees**: Beautiful in autumn; provide shade
- **Birch trees**: White bark; used for furniture

## C 树木的故事

### 阅读文章
Trees have been part of human civilization for thousands of years.

#### 文章标题：The Gift of Trees

Trees are one of nature's greatest gifts. They have provided humans with food, shelter, and materials for thousands of years. From the mighty oak to the delicate cherry blossom, trees play an important role in our world.

Trees clean our air by absorbing carbon dioxide and producing oxygen. They prevent soil erosion and help regulate water cycles. Trees also provide habitats for countless animals and plants. Without trees, life on Earth would be very different.

In many cultures, trees hold special significance. Some trees are considered sacred. Others are symbols of strength, wisdom, or peace. The Christmas tree, for example, is a symbol of hope and renewal.

Today, forests are disappearing at an alarming rate. We must protect our trees and plant new ones. By planting trees, we can help ensure a healthier future for our planet and for generations to come.

### 理解检测
Why are trees important to us?
**Trees provide oxygen, food, shelter, and materials. They also help regulate the environment and provide habitats for animals.**

## D 树木保护

### 写作范例
Write an article about the importance of protecting trees.

#### 文章标题：Protecting Our Trees

Our trees are in danger. Every day, thousands of trees are cut down for timber, agriculture, and development. This is causing serious environmental problems.

We must take action to protect our trees. We can:
1. Support forest conservation efforts
2. Plant new trees in our communities
3. Use sustainable wood products
4. Reduce paper consumption
5. Educate others about the importance of trees

By working together, we can ensure that future generations will enjoy the benefits of trees.

## Discovery 世界著名的树

### 1. 日本樱花树
Cherry blossom trees are symbols of spring and renewal in Japan. They bloom for only a short time each year, attracting millions of visitors.

### 2. 美国红杉
Redwoods are the tallest trees on Earth. Some are over 2,000 years old. They are found in California.

### 3. 非洲猴面包树
Baobab trees can live for thousands of years. They store water in their trunks and are important to African ecosystems.

### 4. 中国银杏树
Ginkgo trees are ancient trees that have survived for millions of years. They are symbols of longevity and resilience.

## 语法主题：数词 (Numerals)

### 基数词 (Cardinal Numbers)
| 数字 | 英文 | 数字 | 英文 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 3 | three | 100 | one hundred |
| 11 | eleven | 375 | three hundred and seventy-five |
| 12 | twelve | 1,000 | one thousand |
| 13 | thirteen | 4,189 | four thousand one hundred and eighty-nine |
| 20 | twenty | 1,000,000 | one million |

### 序数词 (Ordinal Numbers)
| 数字 | 英文 | 数字 | 英文 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 3rd | third | 20th | twentieth |
| 5th | fifth | 21st | twenty-first |
| 8th | eighth | 23rd | twenty-third |
| 13th | thirteenth | 100th | hundredth |

### 用法规则
1. 基数词用于计数
2. 序数词用于表示顺序
3. 小数点后的数字逐个读出
4. 分数用基数词和序数词组合表示

## 练习题

### 练习 1：完成句子

1. **Trees provide oxygen** for us to breathe.
2. **Trees help prevent** soil erosion.
3. **In many cultures, trees hold** special significance.
4. **Today, forests are disappearing** at an alarming rate.
5. **By planting trees, we can help ensure** a healthier future.

### 练习 2：造句

1. 树的作用：
   - Trees provide habitats for animals and plants.

2. 树的象征：
   - The Christmas tree is a symbol of hope and renewal.

3. 保护树木：
   - We must protect our trees and plant new ones.

4. 数词用法：
   - Some redwoods are over 2,000 years old.

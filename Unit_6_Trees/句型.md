## 句型分类学习

### 1. 询问和描述特点句型
- **What is special about...?** - ...有什么特别的？
- **It has...** - 它有...
- **It provides...** - 它提供...

### 2. 表达重要性和作用句型
- **Why are...important?** - ...为什么重要？
- **Trees provide...** - 树提供...
- **Trees help...** - 树帮助...
- **Trees are essential for...** - 树对...至关重要。

### 3. 表达文化和象征意义句型
- **In many cultures...** - 在许多文化中...
- **Some...are considered...** - 有些...被认为是...
- **Others are symbols of...** - 其他的是...的象征。
- **...is a symbol of...** - ...是...的象征。

### 4. 表达现状和必要性句型
- **Today, ...are disappearing...** - 今天，...正在消失...
- **We must...** - 我们必须...
- **By...we can...** - 通过...我们可以...

### 5. 表达行动和合作句型
- **We can support...** - 我们可以支持...
- **We can use...** - 我们可以使用...
- **We can reduce...** - 我们可以减少...
- **By working together...** - 通过一起工作...

## 练习题

### 练习 1：完成句子

1. **What is special about** this tree?
2. **Trees provide oxygen** for us to breathe.
3. **In many cultures, trees hold** special significance.
4. **Today, forests are disappearing** at an alarming rate.
5. **By planting trees, we can help ensure** a healthier future.

### 练习 2：改写句子

1. Trees are important. They provide oxygen.
   → **Trees are important because they provide oxygen.**

2. We should protect trees. We should plant new ones.
   → **We must protect our trees and plant new ones.**

3. Redwoods are tall. Some are over 2,000 years old.
   → **Redwoods are the tallest trees on Earth. Some are over 2,000 years old.**

### 练习 3：造句

1. 询问特点：
   - What is special about the cherry blossom tree?

2. 表达象征：
   - The Christmas tree is a symbol of hope and renewal.

3. 表达行动：
   - We can support forest conservation efforts and plant new trees.

4. 表达合作：
   - By working together, we can ensure a healthier future for all.

## 总结

### 关键句型
- **What is special about...?** - 询问特点
- **Trees provide...** - 表达作用
- **In many cultures...** - 表达文化意义
- **Today, ...are disappearing...** - 表达现状
- **By...we can...** - 表达方式和目标

### 学习建议
1. 多读例句，理解用法
2. 在对话中尝试使用这些句型
3. 做练习题，巩固学习
4. 注意句型的搭配和语境

## A 日常英雄

Lu Yao is looking through the news about "Everyday Heroes" on TEEN-Land website.

**TEEN-Land News | 30 March, Monday | Everyday Heroes**
1. To help out sick people, a 12-year-old girl saved her hair for three years for donation.

3. Teenagers visited people in need to help them rebuild confidence and encourage them never to give up hope.
4. Four children helped pick up cigarette ends in the park to prevent fires.

### A3 听力笔记
Listen to the news report again and complete the notes.

1. When did the two teenagers start to give a helping hand? **Last year**
2. Who received help? **Elderly people who live alone**
3. Why do they need help?
   - They find it difficult to **look after themselves**
   - They are **lonely**
4. What kind of help do they receive? **bring food to them**
5. Who else has joined the programme? **More volunteers**

## B 伸出援手

### 情景导入
The school photography club held a photo contest of school life. Here are the "Star Students of the Week".

### B2 对话学习
Listen to the conversation between Wang Yiming and Lu Yao. How did they ask for and offer help?

> **Yiming**: Hi, Lu Yao! You look a little lost. What are you looking for?
> **Lu Yao**: Hey Yiming! I'm looking for a book of maps for geography class. What are you doing here?
> **Yiming**: I'm a volunteer at the school library.
> **Lu Yao**: Could you please give me a hand?
> **Yiming**: Sure!
> **Lu Yao**: I don't know where to start looking for it. The book is called The World Atlas.
> **Yiming**: Oh! It is on the shelf next to the librarian's desk.
> **Lu Yao**: Thanks. How do I check out a book?
> **Yiming**: Do you have your student card with you?
> **Lu Yao**: Yes, I never come to school without it.
> **Yiming**: Just show the librarian your student card and she'll let you take the book home.
> **Lu Yao**: Thank you for your help.
> **Yiming**: I'm happy to help you out.

### 学习笔记
#### 礼貌地请求帮助：
- Could you please give me a hand?
- Could you help me?
- Can you help me out?

#### 提供帮助：
- Sure! How can I help you?
- I'm happy to help you out.
- I'm glad to help.

## C 紧急情况下的帮助

### 阅读文章
Lu Yao is reading a story about acts of kindness.

#### 文章标题：Rainbow in a cloud

One sunny Saturday morning, as usual, Tom was reading a newspaper to his old neighbour Mrs Bell in her garden. Suddenly Mrs Bell cried out and put her hand to her heart.

"Mrs Bell, are you okay?" asked Tom, very worried.

"I think I'm having a heart attack!" she said in a weak voice. Her face turned pale, and she broke out in a cold sweat.

A heart attack! Tom was shocked, but the eleven-year-old boy learned in a summer camp. In an emergency, you must stay calm. Who could he turn to for help? Mrs Bell lived alone so there was nobody around. His parents went shopping for the old lady.

He quickly took out his phone and called the emergency number. He told the operator clearly where Mrs Bell lived and what had happened. The operator said an ambulance was on its way.

"Tom, get me the blue bottle by my bed," said Mrs Bell. Tom ran into the house and got Mrs Bell's heart pills. She took one.

When the ambulance arrived, two doctors gave Mrs Bell first aid at once. One of them said,"You must be the boy who called us." Tom nodded."Well done" the doctors said.

After they left for the hospital, Tom thought to himself, "Mum often says she is happy to be a rainbow in someone's cloud. Now I seem to understand what she means."

### 理解检测
What two things did Tom do to help Mrs Bell?
1. **He called the emergency number and reported the situation clearly.**
2. **He got Mrs Bell's heart pills and gave them to her.**

## D 感恩

### 写作范例
Read the thank-you letter from Mrs Bell to Tom.

> Dear Tom,
> Thank you very much for your help. You called the emergency number for me and gave me the heart pills just in time. The doctors gave me first aid and they took good care of me. I am feeling much better now. You have no idea how much your help means to me, so please accept my sincere thanks. I'm so happy and grateful to have kind people like you and your parents around me.
> All the best,
> Mrs Bell

### 写作技巧
**感谢信的要素 (Elements of a thank-you letter)**
- Start with a greeting.
- Show your gratitude with one or two details.
- End with your thoughts, feelings or some additional information.

## Discovery 求救信号

### 1. SOS 信号
The best-known signal for help may be "SOS". It's an international signal that can be used in any emergency. It comes from the Morse code, which uses dots and dashes, so it's not the short form of any English words. However, it does have a more secret form -"191519", which are the orders of "S", "O", "S" in the 26 English letters.

### 2. 聋哑人手语
Sign languages allow the deaf-mute to ask for help, but they vary from country to country.
In China, showing your palms out and flapping twice means"help", but in America, you have to put a thumb up on your other palm to deliver the same message.

## 句型分类学习

### 1. 请求帮助句型
- **Could you please give me a hand?** - 你能帮我一把吗？
- **Could you help me?** - 你能帮我吗？
- **Can you help me out?** - 你能帮我解决吗？

### 2. 提供帮助句型
- **Sure! How can I help you?** - 当然！我能怎样帮你？
- **I'm happy to help you out.** - 我很乐意帮你解决。
- **I'm glad to help.** - 我很高兴帮忙。

### 3. 紧急情况句型
- **In an emergency, you must stay calm.** - 在紧急情况下，你必须保持冷静。
- **Who could he turn to for help?** - 他可以向谁求救？
- **An ambulance was on its way.** - 救护车正在赶来。

### 4. 感谢句型
- **Thank you for your help.** - 感谢你的帮助。
- **You have no idea how much your help means to me.** - 你不知道你的帮助对我有多重要。
- **Please accept my sincere thanks.** - 请接受我的真诚感谢。

## 练习题

### 练习 1：完成对话

1. A: **Could you please give me a hand?**
   B: **Sure! How can I help you?**

2. A: **Thank you for your help.**
   B: **I'm happy to help you out.**

3. A: **In an emergency, you must stay calm.**
   B: **You're right. I'll remember that.**

### 练习 2：造句

1. 请求帮助：
   - Could you help me find this book?

2. 提供帮助：
   - I'm glad to help you out.

3. 感谢：
   - Thank you for your help. You have no idea how much it means to me.

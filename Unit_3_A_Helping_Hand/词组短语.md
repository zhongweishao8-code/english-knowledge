### 1. 请求和提供帮助
- **give sb. a hand** - 帮助某人
- **help out** - 帮助解决
- **Could you help me?** - 你能帮我吗？
- **I'm happy to help.** - 我很乐意帮忙。

### 2. 紧急情况
- **in an emergency** - 在紧急情况下
- **stay calm** - 保持冷静
- **call for help** - 求救
- **first aid** - 急救
- **at once** - 立刻

### 3. 身体反应

- **break out in cold sweat** - 冒冷汗
- **cry out** - 大声喊叫
- **put one's hand to** - 把手放在...

- **be grateful to** - 感激

- **accept my sincere thanks** - 接受我的真诚感谢

## 听力原文

### A3 听力笔记：日常英雄

**Listening Task: Complete the notes**

**News Report: Everyday Heroes**

Two teenagers started a volunteer programme last year. They visit elderly people who live alone. These elderly people find it difficult to look after themselves, and they are very lonely. The volunteers bring food to them and keep them company. The programme has been very successful. More and more volunteers have joined the programme. The elderly people are very grateful for the help they receive.

**完成笔记：**
1. When did the two teenagers start to give a helping hand? **Last year**
2. Who received help? **Elderly people who live alone**
3. Why do they need help?
   - They find it difficult to **look after themselves**
   - They are **lonely**
4. What kind of help do they receive? **bring food to them**
5. Who else has joined the programme? **More volunteers**

### B2 对话听力：请求和提供帮助

**Dialogue: Asking for and Offering Help**

> **Yiming**: Hi, Lu Yao! You look a little lost. What are you looking for?
> **Lu Yao**: Hey Yiming! I'm looking for a book of maps for geography class. What are you doing here?
> **Yiming**: I'm a volunteer at the school library.
> **Lu Yao**: Could you please give me a hand?
> **Yiming**: Sure!
> **Lu Yao**: I don't know where to start looking for it. The book is called The World Atlas.
> **Yiming**: Oh! It is on the shelf next to the librarian's desk.
> **Lu Yao**: Thanks. How do I check out a book?
> **Yiming**: Do you have your student card with you?
> **Lu Yao**: Yes, I never come to school without it.
> **Yiming**: Just show the librarian your student card and she'll let you take the book home.
> **Lu Yao**: Thank you for your help.
> **Yiming**: I'm happy to help you out.

**听力理解：**
- Yiming 是图书馆的志愿者
- Lu Yao 在找一本地图书
- Yiming 帮助 Lu Yao 找到了书
- Lu Yao 需要出示学生卡才能借书

## 听力技巧

### 1. 听力前的准备
- 预读题目和选项
- 了解听力内容的主题（帮助、紧急情况）
- 预测可能出现的关键词

### 2. 听力中的技巧
- 集中注意力
- 记下关键信息
- 注意说话人的语调

### 3. 听力后的检查
- 检查答案的完整性
- 确认理解的准确性
- 复习不懂的地方

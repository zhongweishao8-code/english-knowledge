# Unit 2 同义词

## 1. communicate / talk / speak

### 词义辨析
- **communicate**: 正式用语，强调与他人交流信息、想法或情感
- **talk**: 口语化，指人与人之间的交谈
- **speak**: 指说话的动作或能力，可用于正式场合

### 例句
- We need to **communicate** with our parents more often.
- Let's **talk** about the movie last night.
- She can **speak** three languages fluently.

### 练习题
1. I want to _______ with you about my problem. (communicate/talk/speak)
2. Can you _______ English? (talk/speak)
3. They often _______ on the phone. (communicate/talk/speak)

---

## 2. understand / comprehend

### 词义辨析
- **understand**: 常用词，指理解、明白
- **comprehend**: 较正式，指全面理解、领会

### 例句
- I **understand** what you mean.
- It's hard to **comprehend** such complex concepts.

### 练习题
1. Can you _______ this sentence? (understand/comprehend)
2. She couldn't _______ why he was angry. (understand/comprehend)

---

## 3. different / various

### 词义辨析
- **different**: 强调与其他的差异
- **various**: 指多种多样的，强调多样性

### 例句
- This book is **different** from that one.
- There are **various** ways to solve this problem.

### 练习题
1. We have _______ opinions on this matter. (different/various)
2. The restaurant offers _______ dishes. (different/various)

---

## 4. mistake / error

### 词义辨析
- **mistake**: 常指因疏忽或误解造成的错误
- **error**: 更正式，可指任何错误，含道德或技术错误

### 例句
- I made a **mistake** in my homework.
- This is an **error** in the computer system.

### 练习题
1. It's a serious _______ to lie. (mistake/error)
2. Don't worry, everyone makes _______ sometimes. (mistakes/errors)

---

## 5. apologise / say sorry

### 词义辨析
- **apologise**: 正式用语，指道歉
- **say sorry**: 口语化，更随意

### 例句
- I **apologise** for being late.
- You should **say sorry** to her.

### 练习题
1. He _______ for his rudeness. (apologised/said sorry)
2. It's never too late to _______ (apologise/say sorry)

---

## 参考答案
1. communicate 2. speak 3. talk 4. understand 5. comprehend 6. different 7. various 8. error 9. mistakes 10. apologized/apologised 11. say sorry/apologise

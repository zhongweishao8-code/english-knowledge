## 词汇学习重点

### 1. 交流方式相关词汇
- **in person** - 亲自；当面
- **text** - 发短信
- **chat** - 聊天
- **communicate** - 交流
- **social media** - 社交媒体
- **emoji** - 表情符号

### 2. 文化差异相关词汇
- **accent** - 口音
- **clothing** - 衣服
- **customs** - 习俗
- **traditions** - 传统
- **gesture** - 手势
- **cultural** - 文化的

### 3. 交流问题相关词汇
- **misunderstand** - 误解
- **embarrassment** - 尴尬
- **rude** - 粗鲁的
- **apologise** - 道歉
- **fault** - 过错

### 4. 关键短语
- **in person** - 亲自
- **in addition** - 另外
- **based on** - 基于
- **cheer for** - 为...加油
- **go ahead** - 继续
- **a bit** - 有点儿

## 词汇填空练习

### 练习 1：选择合适的词汇

1. The **survey** shows that most students prefer texting.
2. **In person** communication is still the most popular.
3. **Emojis** help us express our feelings.
4. I **apologised** for misunderstanding you.
5. **Cultural** differences can cause misunderstandings.

### 练习 2：用词汇完成句子

1. We should **avoid** embarrassment in communication.
2. **In addition** to accents, clothing also differs.
3. I **realised** that differences can bridge cultures.
4. They were **interested** in learning about Chinese culture.
5. **Effective** communication is important for understanding.

## 交流方式词汇扩展

### 面对面交流 (Face-to-face Communication)
- talk in person - 面对面交谈
- have a conversation - 进行对话
- meet face to face - 面对面见面

### 远程交流 (Remote Communication)
- phone call - 电话
- video chat - 视频聊天
- text message - 短信
- email - 电子邮件
- social media - 社交媒体

### 非语言交流 (Non-verbal Communication)
- gesture - 手势
- facial expression - 面部表情
- body language - 肢体语言
- eye contact - 眼神接触

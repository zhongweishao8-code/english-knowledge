## A2 听力练习：交流方式匹配

### 听力文本

**Student 1: Wang Yiming**
I like texting because it's convenient. I can send messages anytime, anywhere. And I can read the messages again and again if I don't understand them the first time. Texting also allows me to think about what I want to say before I send it.

**Student 2: Lin Dong**
I prefer video-chatting. It's great because I can see my friends' faces and hear their voices. I get immediate feedback, and we can see each other's expressions. It's almost like talking in person, but we can do it from home.

**Student 3: Li Bailing**
I like texting too, but I also enjoy video-chatting. Texting is convenient, and emojis help me express my feelings. But sometimes I want to see my friends' faces, so I video-chat with them.

**Lu Yao**: Texting is my favorite way to communicate. It allows me to **express myself** clearly. I can read the messages **again and again** if I need to. This helps me understand what my friends are saying.

## B2 对话听力：文化差异和误解

### 听力文本

> **Harry**: Hey, Bailing. You seemed unhappy with me these days. What happened?
> **Bailing**: I just...I don't know how to say it. But I thought you were a bit rude to me.
> **Harry**: Why do you say so?
> **Bailing**: Do you remember the performance last Friday? I was cheering for you, but you pointed your finger at me. Were you angry with me?
> **Harry**: I'm really sorry. But I didn't mean to do that. I just wanted to invite you to sing with me.
> **Bailing**: It doesn't matter. I apologise for misunderstanding you. In our culture, we usually use a sweep of the open hand - palm upwards.
> **Harry**: I see. I'll keep that in mind.

### 听力理解

**问题 1**: What was the misunderstanding between Harry and Bailing?
**答案**: Bailing thought Harry was rude because he pointed his finger at her. But Harry was actually trying to invite her to sing with him.

**问题 2**: What did Bailing explain about cultural differences?
**答案**: In Chinese culture, people use a sweep of the open hand with palm upwards to invite someone, not pointing a finger.

**问题 3**: How did Harry respond?
**答案**: Harry apologised and said he would keep this in mind for the future.

## 听力技巧

### 1. 听力前的准备
- 预读题目和选项
- 了解听力内容的主题（交流方式、文化差异）
- 预测可能出现的关键词

### 2. 听力中的技巧
- 集中注意力，不要分心
- 记下关键信息（学生名字、交流方式、原因）
- 注意说话人的语调和重音

### 3. 听力后的检查
- 检查答案的完整性
- 确认理解的准确性
- 复习不懂的地方

- **video-chatting** - 视频聊天
- **talking on the phone** - 通话

- **misunderstanding** - 误解

## 常见听力问题

### 问题 1：听不懂某些单词
**解决方案**：
- 根据上下文猜测意思
- 不要停下来，继续听
- 听完后查字典

### 问题 2：跟不上说话速度
**解决方案**：
- 多听几遍
- 逐句听，不要一次听整段
- 看文本，同时听音频

### 问题 3：记不住信息
**解决方案**：
- 做笔记
- 使用表格或图表
- 听完后立即复述

## 听力材料补充

### 交流方式的优缺点

**Texting**
- 优点：convenient, can read again, time to think
- 缺点：no immediate feedback, can be misunderstood

**Video-chatting**
- 优点：see faces, hear voices, immediate feedback
- 缺点：need internet, need device

**In person**
- 优点：most direct, can see body language
- 缺点：need to be in same place, takes time

**Phone call**
- 优点：immediate, can hear tone of voice
- 缺点：can't see faces, no written record

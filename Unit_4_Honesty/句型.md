## 句型分类学习

### 1. 选择疑问句
- **Did you make up a story or tell the truth?** - 你是编造故事还是说实话？
- **Did you take his offer or turn it down?** - 你是接受还是拒绝？
- **Which story do you prefer, A or B?** - 你更喜欢哪个？

### 2. 请求建议句型
- **What should I do to make it right?** - 我应该怎样做才能弥补？
- **You should be honest with your friend.** - 你应该对你的朋友诚实。
- **You should tell the truth to your teacher.** - 你应该向你的老师说实话。

### 3. 道歉句型
- **I'm so sorry!** - 我很抱歉！
- **I'm truly sorry.** - 我真的很抱歉。
- **I didn't mean to...** - 我不是故意...
- **I was careless.** - 我很粗心。

### 4. 接受道歉句型
- **I guess accidents sometimes happen.** - 我想意外有时会发生。
- **I know you didn't mean it.** - 我知道你不是故意的。
- **Don't worry about it.** - 别担心。
- **That's OK.** - 没关系。

## 练习题

### 练习 1：完成对话

1. A: **Did you make up a story or tell the truth?**
   B: **I told the truth.**

2. A: **I'm so sorry! I accidentally spilled water on your book.**
   B: **I guess accidents sometimes happen. I know you didn't mean it.**

3. A: **What should I do to make it right?**
   B: **You should be honest with your friend and apologise.**

### 练习 2：造句

1. 选择疑问句：
   - Did you copy the homework or do it yourself?

2. 道歉：
   - I'm truly sorry. I didn't mean to hurt your feelings.

3. 接受道歉：
   - Don't worry about it. I know you didn't mean it.
